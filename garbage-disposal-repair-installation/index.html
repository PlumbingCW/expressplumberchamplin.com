Urgent situations

Common disposal problems

Humming but not turning

A jam is the usual cause. A plumber can free the flywheel from below.

Dead, no sound at all

A tripped reset button, a tripped breaker or a failed motor can be the cause.

Water under the sink

A leak at the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet or the housing can all look the same.

A sink that drains slowly

Buildup in the trap or the drain line downstream of the disposal can cause a backup.

Why they jam

What a disposal handles and what it does not

InSinkErator, a major maker, advises running cold water while the disposal runs and for about 15 seconds afterward to flush waste through the drain. The same guidance says to avoid putting grease, fats and oils down the drain. These simple habits prevent most clogs.

Fibrous foods such as celery and corn husks, starchy items such as potato peels in quantity, coffee grounds in bulk and bones are common culprits. Grease cools and coats the pipe, which is why a kitchen drain that keeps backing up may need a deeper cleaning. See hydro jetting for lines with buildup.

Repair or replace

Repair or replace the disposal

Repair fits when

  • A jam is the only problem.
  • The leak is at a seal or the sink flange.
  • The unit is fairly new.
  • The motor spins freely once cleared.

Replacement fits when

  • The housing is cracked or corroded.
  • The motor is burned out.
  • Jams keep returning.
  • The unit is old and undersized for your use.
Installation

What a disposal installation involves

Disconnect

Power is shut off and the old unit is removed from the sink flange.

Mount

The new mounting assembly is attached under the sink.

Connect

The drain, the dishwasher inlet if present and the power are connected.

Test

We run water and the unit to check for leaks and noise.

Installation needs a nearby outlet or a hardwired connection, and the drain must be compatible. Tell us whether you have a dishwasher so we can plan the knockout plug and the connection.

Related work

When the sink drain is the real problem

Slow kitchen drains are not always the disposal. A partial clog in the drain line, a lack of venting or buildup in the line may be to blame. Drain cleaning clears the line, and a leaky faucet or supply line near the sink is a separate fix. See faucet repair for that.

Care

Care guidelines from the maker

According to InSinkErator's care guidance, you should keep grease, fats and oils out of the drain and run cold water during and after use. A disposal that is used well lasts longer and jams less. The guidance also recommends against putting large bones and fibrous scraps in the unit.

Questions About Garbage Disposal Repair & Installation

Do you handle disposal emergencies at night or on weekends in Champlin?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. We book same-day appointments when available and tell you the price before work starts.

My disposal hums but will not spin. What is wrong?

It is usually jammed. Turn off the power and call. A plumber can free the flywheel safely.

Can I put grease down the disposal?

No. Grease and oils coat the drain and cause backups. InSinkErator advises avoiding them.

Should I run water while it works?

Yes. Cold water during use and for about 15 seconds afterward helps flush waste.

Can you install a disposal I bought?

Yes. Tell us the model and whether you have a dishwasher.
