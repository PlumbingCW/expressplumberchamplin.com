Brooklyn Park is Minnesota's fifth largest city, and its homes range from 1970s split levels to newer townhomes. We take emergency plumbing calls here at any hour and book same-day appointments when available. Because the city borders Champlin, our crews are never far away.

Housing

A city of 1970s and 1980s homes

Brooklyn Park had 86,478 residents in 2020. Most homes were built between 1970 and 1989, and the median build year is 1977. That age matters for plumbing. Water heaters have usually been replaced once already, cast iron drain lines can be showing their age, and older supply pipe may be due for attention.

Neighborhoods such as Crescent Ridge, Cherokee, Colorado, Fairway Estates and Woodland Trails are typical of the era, and we work in all of them.

Sewer backups

When a sewer backup hits a Brooklyn Park home

The City of Brooklyn Park runs a sewer backup line at 763-493-8007. If sewage is coming up in your basement, call the city to ask whether the public main is involved, and call us to clear and inspect your own line. The two calls answer different questions, and the camera footage helps you with both.

See sewer line cleaning for how we clear a main line and sewer line repair if the camera shows damage.

Water

Groundwater supply and what it means

Brooklyn Park draws its drinking water from groundwater. The city's reports do not give a single hardness number we can quote with confidence, so a tap test is the honest first step before you spend on a softener. If you do find scale on faucets and in the water heater, our softener page explains the decision.
