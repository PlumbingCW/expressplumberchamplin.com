Before you call

Homes this new may still have warranty coverage from the builder or from manufacturers. Using an outside plumber for a covered item can complicate a claim, so read your paperwork before you book any repair.

We cannot see your warranty terms, and we do not know what D.R. Horton covers in this community. Your closing documents or the builder's warranty office can tell you what is covered and for how long.

General plumbing information

Everyday plumbing in newer two-story homes

Newer homes still need ordinary care. Water heaters collect sediment over time, drains pick up hair and grease, and faucet cartridges wear out. Two-story layouts put bathrooms upstairs, so a leak there can show up as a stain on the ceiling below.

Flushing the tank on a schedule and knowing where the main shutoff is cover most of what an owner can do.

Hot water

No hot water or a leak at the tank is the most common urgent call. See water heater repair.

Slow drains

Kitchen and bath drains slow down from buildup. See drain cleaning.

Fixtures

Dripping faucets and failed shutoff valves are usually quick repairs. See faucet and sink repair.

Toilets

Running or leaking toilets waste water fast. See toilet repair.

Simple habits

Habits that keep a newer home trouble-free

  • Learn where the main water shutoff is before you need it.
  • Look under each sink and around the water heater a few times a year.
  • Keep grease, wipes and paper towels out of the drains.
  • Ask the plumber what a water heater flush involves and how often it makes sense for your water.
