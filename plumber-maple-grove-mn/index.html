Before we arrive

Before We Arrive in Maple Grove

Know your shutoff

The main valve is near the water meter or where the line enters the house.

Water heater leaks

Turn off the gas or the breaker to the heater, then close its cold-water valve.

Clear the area

Move belongings away from the water and the work path.

Take photos

Capture the damage for your insurer before cleanup.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or CenterPoint at 612-372-5050 first.
