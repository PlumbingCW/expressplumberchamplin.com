Urgent situations

Sewer backup: when jetting is the next step

Jetting is the answer when the same line has been snaked and clogged again, when a cable stalled partway, or when a camera shows heavy buildup. Callers describe it in familiar ways. Someone snaked it and the cable got stuck.

The drain slows every few weeks. Roots keep returning.

The cable stopped short

A snake that stalls partway may be stuck on roots, scale or a pipe defect. A camera clarifies which, and jetting can clear what a cable cannot.

Clogs return within weeks

A cable opens a channel through grease. Jetting scours the pipe wall, so the line holds its full diameter longer.

Roots in the line

Roots grow through joints and trap everything that flows past. Jetting with a cutting nozzle clears them, though repair may still be needed.

Backup in a lower drain

Water in a basement floor drain or tub on a main-level line means the main is blocked, and the fastest fix is often jetting.

How it works

What hydro jetting does inside the pipe

Camera first

A sewer camera shows the blockage and the pipe condition before pressure is applied.

Pick the nozzle

A nozzle is chosen for grease, scale or roots, and pressure is set for the pipe material.

Jet the line

The hose feeds in through a cleanout and water scours the walls as the nozzle is pulled back.

Recheck

A second camera pass confirms the line is clear and shows any damage that cleaning exposed.

The main limit is the pipe. Jetting suits sound pipe. A line that is cracked, collapsed or badly offset can be damaged by pressure, which is why the camera comes first. If the camera shows damage, we explain it and discuss sewer line repair before cleaning further.

Where it helps most

Grease, scale and roots in Champlin homes and shops

Kitchen lines carry grease that cools and sticks, and older cast iron develops scale that narrows the bore. Both respond well to jetting. Businesses in Champlin's commercial areas along Highway 169 and in business parks see the same buildup from restaurant and floor-drain use, and the commercial plumbing page covers after-hours calls.

FAQs

Questions About Hydro Jetting

Do you handle hydro jetting emergencies at night or on weekends in Champlin?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. We book same-day appointments when available and tell you the price before work starts.

Is hydro jetting safe for old pipes?

It is safe for sound pipe. It can damage a cracked or collapsed pipe, so a camera inspection comes first and we stop if the pipe cannot take pressure.

How is jetting different from snaking?

A snake pushes through a blockage and leaves a channel. Jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the whole pipe wall, which helps when clogs keep returning.

My cable got stuck partway. What now?

A camera can show whether roots, scale or a damaged pipe stopped it. Jetting may clear it, or repair may be the next step.

Will jetting stop roots permanently?

No. It clears the roots that are in the pipe, but they can return if a joint leaks. Repair or lining addresses the entry point.
