Tankless Water Heater Code 11 in Champlin: What to Check

Code 11 appears on several tankless brands and points at ignition. Here is what it means and what to check safely.

Written by the Champlin Express Plumbing team · Published 2026 · 8 min read

Call now

A tankless water heater that stops with a code on the display is a stressful moment, especially when the shower is cold. Code 11 is one of the most common, and it appears on more than one brand. It usually relates to ignition, though the exact meaning varies.

This guide explains what the code means, which checks are safe for a homeowner and when to call a plumber.

What it means

Code 11 by brand

BrandCode 11 or equivalentMeaning
RinnaiCode 11No ignition
NoritzCode 11Air, spark or gas, the elements needed for ignition, are not all present
TakagiIgnition failure (three flashes)The burner did not light
NavienSee the manualCheck the model's own code list

Rinnai's documentation notes that a loose flame rod can contribute to the fault. Always check the manual for your specific model, since codes differ by series.

Safe checks

What you can try first

Check the gas supply

Make sure the gas valve to the unit is open and other gas appliances work.

Check the vent

Look at the vent termination outside for snow, ice, nests or debris.

Reset the unit

Turn the power off for a minute, then back on, and try the hot water.

Check the inlet filter

A clogged water inlet filter can reduce flow and prevent firing, and cleaning it is within reach for many owners.

If the code returns after one reset, stop. Repeated attempts do not help and can be unsafe if gas is not burning correctly.

Gas safety

If you smell gas

Leave the building and call 911 or the gas utility. In the Champlin area, CenterPoint Energy lists 612-372-5050 and asks you to press 1 for a leak. Do not use switches or phones near the unit. The Minnesota Department of Public Safety's pipeline safety guidance tells you to leave and call from a safe place.

Typical causes

What a plumber finds

Fuel and air causes

  • Gas valve closed or low gas pressure.
  • A blocked vent or intake.
  • Propane tank low.
  • Flame rod or igniter wear.

Maintenance causes

  • Scale in the heat exchanger.
  • A clogged inlet filter.
  • A dirty burner.
  • Neglected annual flushing.

Scale is a common root cause because it forces a unit to work harder and fail more often. According to Navien's manual, flushing depends on water hardness, and it recommends annual flushing for moderately hard water without a treatment device. Rinnai's manual says hard water may need more frequent flushing. See tankless water heater service for flushing and repair.

Repair or replace

Is it time for a new unit

A single ignition fault on a clean, well-maintained unit is usually a small repair. Repeated faults, a cracked heat exchanger or severe scale can mean replacement is the better value. A technician checks the combustion, the heat exchanger and the error history before recommending either.

Champlin note

A Champlin note

Champlin's water comes from six city wells, and the city's report does not give a hardness number. Test your tap before choosing a flush schedule or a softener. If your water is hard, a softener upstream protects the heat exchanger, and our water heater repair page also covers tank units that face the same scale.

Sizing and flow

Why flow and sizing matter for ignition

A tankless unit lights only when it senses enough water flow. If flow is too low, because a filter is clogged, a faucet aerator is blocked or a valve is partly closed, the unit may not fire. The result can look like an ignition fault even though gas and spark are fine.

Try the hot tap at full flow and then at a trickle. If the unit lights at full flow and not at a trickle, the problem is a low-flow shutoff, which is by design. If it does not light at either, ignition is the suspect.

What to do

What to do while you wait for service

Close the hot water valve at fixtures you are not using, and keep the electric power on, since many units need it to run freeze protection. If you have a recreational vehicle style backup, or a kettle and a stove, use those for urgent needs.

If you notice a gas smell, soot around the unit or a yellow flame, shut the gas valve, leave the room and call for help. Any unit that shows these signs should stay off until a technician looks at it.

Prevent a repeat

Keep the unit healthy

  • Flush the unit on the schedule in your manual.
  • Clean the inlet filter regularly.
  • Keep the vent termination clear of snow and debris.
  • Install service valves if the unit lacks them.
  • Have the unit inspected yearly.

FAQs

Tankless Questions in Champlin

What does code 11 mean on a Rinnai?

Rinnai defines it as no ignition.

Can I keep resetting the unit?

Reset once. If the code returns, call a plumber.

Does hard water cause tankless faults?

Scale can strain the unit. Follow the manual's flushing schedule and test your tap.

What if I smell gas?

Leave and call 911 or CenterPoint at 612-372-5050, pressing 1 for a leak.

Should I replace a unit that shows code 11?

Not necessarily. Many ignition faults are small repairs.
