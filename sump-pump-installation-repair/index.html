When a sump pump stops, the basement can start to fill within hours of heavy rain or snowmelt. Our line is open 24/7 and we book same-day appointments when available.

If water is rising, keep electricity away from standing water. Do not walk into a flooded basement when outlets or the panel might be wet. Move valuables upward, and if the basement is flooding with the pump failed, call us right away.

Urgent situations

Signs a sump pump is failing

The pump runs but water is not leaving

A failed check valve, a clogged discharge or a worn impeller can leave the pump spinning without moving water.

The pump never runs

A stuck float, a tripped breaker or a dead motor can leave the pit full.

Loud grinding or rattling

A worn bearing or debris in the impeller often precedes failure.

It runs constantly

A stuck float, a pit too small for the inflow or a failed check valve causes short cycling.

Discharge rules

Where the water is allowed to go in Champlin

The City of Champlin's code (Sec. 58-270) directs sump pump discharge to lawn areas outside the right of way. It should not go to the sidewalk or the street. The Metropolitan Council says discharge belongs on the ground, not in the sanitary sewer, because clear water overloads the wastewater system.

That is why pumping into a floor drain is a problem, and why we route discharge to the lawn on installation. Winter adds a challenge, because discharge pipe outside can freeze. We plan the route, the slope and the outlet to reduce that risk.

Repair or replace

Repair, replace or add a backup

Repair fits when

  • The motor is sound.
  • The float or the check valve failed.
  • The pit and discharge are in good shape.
  • The pump is fairly new.

Replacement fits when

  • The motor is burned out.
  • The pump is old and short cycling.
  • The capacity is too small.
  • The pit is cracked or undersized.

Many basement floods happen during storms that also knock out power. A battery backup pump runs when the main pump or the power fails. A water-powered backup uses municipal water pressure and has its own limits. We explain what fits your home, and you decide.

Installation

What a new sump pump installation involves

Set the pit

A pit is cut through the slab or an existing basin is cleaned and checked.

Install the pump

The pump, check valve and float are set and the discharge is connected.

Route discharge

Piping runs to a legal outlet on the lawn with a proper slope.

Test

We fill the pit to confirm the pump starts, moves water and shuts off.

A new pit also affects your basement. For ongoing seepage or a damp floor, see basement waterproofing, since a sump pump alone does not seal a wall.

Champlin context

Water table and low ground near the river

The DNR describes the Anoka Sand Plain as level to gently rolling, with poorly drained soils in places. Homes near the Mississippi and the creeks that feed it can see a higher water table in wet seasons. The city's rebuilt Mill Pond dam is expected to remove 60 acres from the floodplain, but that does not replace a working pump in your own basement.

Standards

What the code and the city say

According to the City of Champlin's code (Sec. 58-270), sump pump discharge goes to lawn areas outside the right of way. According to the Metropolitan Council, it belongs on the ground and not in the sanitary sewer. A compliant installation follows both.
