Baking Soda on a Clogged Drain: What It Clears in Champlin

It fizzes and it feels like cleaning. Here is the honest answer on what it clears and what it cannot.

Written by the Champlin Express Plumbing team · Published 2026 · 7 min read

Baking soda and vinegar is the best-known home drain remedy. It is cheap, it fizzes and it is far gentler than a bottle of caustic cleaner. Whether it works depends on what is in the pipe.

This guide is honest about what the method can and cannot do, so you do not waste a Saturday on a drain that needs something else.

The chemistry

What actually happens

Baking soda is a mild base and vinegar is a weak acid. Mixed, they produce carbon dioxide bubbles, water and a salt. The fizz can loosen light film on a pipe and can freshen a drain. It has little force and does not dissolve hair, dense grease or a solid obstruction.

Hot water follows the mixture in the usual method. Very hot water can soften grease. Boiling water can crack a porcelain bowl, and it can soften PVC joints, so use hot tap water on plastic pipes and avoid pouring boiling water into a toilet.

Where it helps

Jobs it can do

  • Freshen a drain that smells because of light film.
  • Help clear a slow bathroom sink with soap buildup.
  • Maintain a drain that is already flowing.
Where it does not

Jobs it cannot do

Baking soda helps with

  • Light odors.
  • Soap film in a slow sink.
  • Routine maintenance.
  • A mild early slowdown.

Baking soda cannot fix

  • A hair clog in a tub.
  • Dense grease in a kitchen line.
  • A blocked main line.
  • Roots, a sag or a broken pipe.
Steps

How to try it properly

Remove standing water

Bail out what you can so the mixture reaches the clog.

Add baking soda

Pour about half a cup into the drain.

Add vinegar

Follow with about half a cup of vinegar and cover the drain.

Flush

After about 15 minutes, flush with hot tap water.

If the drain is still slow after two tries, the clog is not the kind this method handles. A plunger or a hand auger is the next step, and after that it is time for a plumber. See drain cleaning for how we choose between cabling and jetting.

Safety

Do not mix with chemical cleaners

Never pour baking soda and vinegar after a commercial drain cleaner. Mixed products can react unpredictably, and the pipe may hold a pool of caustic chemical. If you have used a cleaner, tell the plumber.

The bigger picture

Why drains clog again

A drain that clogs again and again is telling you something. Grease coating a kitchen line, scale from hard water or a defect in the main line returns the clog even after cleaning. Hydro jetting restores the full pipe diameter for lines with buildup.

Champlin note

A Champlin note

Champlin's drinking water comes from six city wells, and the city's report does not give a hardness value. If your drains and fixtures scale up, test the tap before deciding on a softener.

Prevention

Habits that keep drains open

Most clogs build slowly, so small habits matter more than any treatment. According to InSinkErator's care guidance, grease, fats and oils should stay out of the drain, and the same advice applies to every kitchen line, not only those with a disposal. Let cooking fat cool in a can and put it in the trash.

In bathrooms, a hair catcher over the tub drain stops most clogs before they start. Clean it weekly, since a full catcher does nothing. A bathroom sink benefits from a stopper you can lift out, because hair and soap collect on the stopper mechanism more than in the pipe.

  • Scrape plates into the trash before rinsing them.
  • Use a mesh strainer in the kitchen sink and empty it daily.
  • Run hot tap water for a minute after washing greasy dishes.
  • Keep wipes, cotton swabs and floss out of toilets and sinks.
  • Flush a bathroom drain with hot tap water once a month.
What to do

What to do when a drain stops completely

When a drain stops completely, stop adding water. Every gallon you send down only raises the level in the fixture. Bail out standing water with a cup, plunge with a good seal, and try a hand auger if you own one.

If water appears in another fixture while you plunge, the blockage is in the shared line, and further effort at the sink will not help. That is the moment to call, because the main line needs a cable machine, and a plumber can also tell you whether the pipe itself is the problem.

FAQs

Drain Cleaning Questions in Champlin

Does baking soda and vinegar unclog a drain?

It can help with light film and odors. It does not clear dense clogs.

Can I use boiling water on PVC pipes?

Avoid it. Very hot water can soften joints. Use hot tap water.

Is it safe after using a chemical drain cleaner?

No. Do not mix products. Tell the plumber what you used.

What should I try after baking soda fails?

A plunger or hand auger, then a plumber.

Why do my drains scale up?

Hard water can leave deposits. Test your tap, since the city's report gives no hardness value.
