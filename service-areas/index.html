Champlin

Home base: Champlin, MN

Champlin sits where the Crow and Mississippi rivers meet, in Hennepin County. According to the City of Champlin, its Utilities Department runs the water and sanitary sewer, and the city draws its water from six wells. See our drain cleaning and leak detection pages for the most common calls here.

Dayton

Dayton and the northwest edge

Dayton lies northwest of Champlin, where the Crow and Mississippi rivers meet. Part of the city has municipal water, and the rest relies on private wells and septic systems. We repair household plumbing in both cases and say so when a problem belongs with a well or septic contractor.

Brooklyn Park

Brooklyn Park to the south

Brooklyn Park borders Champlin and has many homes built in the 1970s and 1980s. Water heaters, main drains and sump pumps are the most common calls. The city runs its own sewer backup line, and we explain when to call it.

Maple Grove

Maple Grove to the southwest

Maple Grove has two ZIP codes, 55311 and 55369, and the second lists Osseo as its preferred name. We serve both. Calls here run from water heaters in newer homes to frozen pipes in cold snaps.

Anoka

Anoka across the river

Anoka sits across the Mississippi from Champlin and links to our side by the Anoka-Champlin bridge. Older homes near downtown bring slow main drains, aging supply lines and basements near the rivers.

Coon Rapids

Coon Rapids to the east

Coon Rapids is a large Anoka County city with treated well water and many homes near the river terraces. Main line backups, water heater failures and sump pumps lead the list.

ZIP codes

How coverage works

Each town page lists its main ZIP codes. We also take calls in ZIP codes within about 10 miles of the town centers, and we confirm your address when you call. If your town is not listed, call anyway, and we tell you if we can reach you.

Winter and storms

Seasons that keep us busy

Cold snaps bring burst pipes and frozen outdoor faucets, and spring melt and summer storms bring sump pump failures and basement water. Water heaters tend to fail without warning at any time of year. The line is open for all of them. See burst pipe repair for winter calls.

Giving directions

Help us find you quickly

When you call, give us your street address, the nearest cross street and your ZIP code. If you live on a long rural road or in a new development whose streets may not appear on every map, tell us a landmark. We plan the route and give you an estimated window.
